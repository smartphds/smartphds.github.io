Where PhDs land

Industry has more doors than "scientist."

Most PhDs apply only to bench roles. These functions hire PhDs, pay well, and value the rigor you built in grad school.

Regulatory Affairs

Turn data into submissions

Write and defend the CMC and clinical sections of IND, NDA and BLA filings.

Quality

Own GMP compliance

Review methods, investigate deviations and keep products release-ready.

Analytical Development

Build and validate methods

Develop HPLC, MS and biophysical assays that hold up under ICH Q2.

Medical Science Liaison

Talk science with physicians

Field-based role explaining clinical data to key opinion leaders.

Medical Writing

Write what regulators read

Protocols, clinical study reports and lay summaries of trial results.

CMC & Tech Transfer

Move drugs to manufacturing

Coordinate CDMOs, scale-up and the chemistry behind every batch.
